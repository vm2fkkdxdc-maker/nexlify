Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Effective date: May 30, 2026

This DPA applies where Clutch processes personal data on behalf of customer organizations, including public entities.

Roles

Customer is the controller (or business) and Clutch is the processor (or service provider), except where otherwise stated by law.

Processing Terms

  • Processing is limited to documented customer instructions.
  • Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards are maintained.
  • Subprocessors are managed under written obligations.
  • Assistance is provided for data subject requests and breach response.

Cross-Border Transfers

Where required, lawful transfer mechanisms are implemented for international data movement.
